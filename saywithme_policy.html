Privacy Policy for Say With Me

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Thank you for using Say With Me.

Say With Me is an offline language-learning and communication app. It is designed to keep the learning experience on your device. The app does not require an account and does not upload your photos, camera frames, voice recordings, cards, labels, or learning progress to our servers.

Information We Collect

Say With Me does not collect personal information, run analytics, show advertising, use tracking technologies, or maintain a user account system.

The app does not collect or receive from you:

  • Names, email addresses, or phone numbers
  • Location information or device identifiers
  • Advertising identifiers or usage analytics
  • Biometric information
  • Photos or images for a remote service
  • Voice recordings for storage or upload

Camera and Photo Access

The camera is used to recognize objects and display their names. Camera frames are processed on your device by the bundled image-labeling model. Say With Me does not upload camera frames, send them to a cloud service, or share them with us or any third party.

You may also take a photo or choose one from your photo library to create a personal word card. If you save that card, the image and its labels are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. These photos are never uploaded, transmitted, or shared by Say With Me. Removing the app or clearing its app storage may permanently delete those local files.

Microphone and Speech Recognition

Microphone access is used only when you practice saying a word. Say With Me does not save, upload, or share your recordings. The operating system and its speech-recognition provider may process audio according to their own privacy settings and terms; Say With Me does not control or store that system-level processing.

Text-to-Speech

Spoken labels and phrases use the text-to-speech engine installed on your device. Say With Me does not send the text to its own server or store voice output.

Local Storage

The app may store settings, word cards, translations, photos belonging to those cards, and learning progress locally on your device. Say With Me does not use cloud storage, external databases, or remote servers for this data.

Third-Party Services

Say With Me does not use advertising networks, analytics services, tracking technologies, social features, remote logging, or crash-reporting services. The app uses operating-system camera, photo-library, speech-recognition, and text-to-speech capabilities when you choose features that need them.

Children's Privacy

Say With Me is designed as an educational aid for children and families. Because the app does not collect personal information or maintain accounts, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children. A parent or caregiver should stay nearby and choose activities appropriate for the child.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If this Privacy Policy changes, the updated version will be made available in the app or through the app store listing. The "Last updated" date above will also be changed.
