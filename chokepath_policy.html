Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 21, 2026

📋 Overview

Choke Path ("the App") is a tower-defense style game designed to be played entirely offline. We respect your privacy and are committed to keeping the App simple and private.

The App does not collect, store, process, transmit, or share any personal data or other user data.

🔒 Data Collection

We do not collect or store any information about you, your device, or your activity in the App. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, or other contact information
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • IP addresses or network information
  • Location data
  • Gameplay statistics or usage analytics
  • Crash reports or diagnostic information
  • Photos, videos, microphone input, or other media
  • Contacts or other personal information from your device
  • Cookies or tracking information

📴 Offline Gameplay

Choke Path is designed to function fully offline. The App does not require an internet connection to play the game and does not communicate with our servers or transmit gameplay data.

No account, registration, login, or user profile is required to use the App.

🛡️ Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, crash-reporting, social-media, or other services that collect or transmit user data.

We do not sell, rent, or share user data with third parties because we do not collect any user data.

💾 Local Data

The App may store game-related information locally on your device, such as game progress, settings, or preferences, when required for the game to function.

This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to us or any third party. We do not have access to locally stored game data.

📱 App Permissions

Choke Path does not require access to personal or sensitive device features such as:

  • Camera
  • Microphone
  • Contacts
  • Location
  • Photos or media

The App is intended to operate without an internet connection or access to personal device data.

🗂️ Data Retention and Deletion

We do not collect or retain any user data on our systems. Any game data stored locally by the App remains on your device.

You can remove locally stored App data by uninstalling the App or using the applicable data-management options provided by your device.

👶 Children's Privacy

Choke Path does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults. Since the App does not collect or transmit personal information, there is no user account or personal profile associated with gameplay.

🔄 Changes to This Policy

If this Privacy Policy is updated, the revised version will be published at this URL with a new effective date.
