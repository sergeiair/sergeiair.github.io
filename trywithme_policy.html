Privacy Policy for TryWithMe

Last updated: July 5, 2026

Thank you for using TryWithMe.

TryWithMe is designed with privacy as a core principle. The application works entirely on your device and does not collect, store, transmit, or share your personal information.

Information Collection

TryWithMe does not collect any personal data.

The app does not collect:

  • Names
  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Location information
  • Device identifiers
  • Usage analytics
  • Crash reports
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Biometric information
  • Voice recordings
  • Images captured by the camera

Camera Access

TryWithMe requires access to your device camera to recognize objects and display their names in different languages.

All image processing happens locally on your device. Images are never uploaded, stored, or transmitted anywhere.

Microphone Access

TryWithMe requires microphone access to allow users to practice pronunciation.

Voice recordings are processed locally on your device for pronunciation comparison purposes only. Recordings are not stored, uploaded, or shared.

Data Storage

All processing performed by TryWithMe happens locally on your device.

The application does not use external servers, cloud services, or third-party databases.

Third-Party Services

TryWithMe does not use:

  • Analytics services
  • Advertising networks
  • Tracking technologies
  • User behavior monitoring
  • Remote logging or crash reporting services

Children's Privacy

Because TryWithMe does not collect any personal information, it does not knowingly collect information from children or any other users.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If this Privacy Policy changes in the future, the updated version will be made available within the application or through the app store listing.
